# Third-party learning content

## Tatoeba A1 phrase banks

The A1 phrase banks for Czech, Danish, Dutch, Filipino, Finnish, Greek,
Hebrew, Indonesian, Norwegian Bokmål, Persian, Polish, Swedish, Swahili,
Thai, Ukrainian and Vietnamese are selected from the [Tatoeba Project]
English parallel exports, downloaded on 2026-08-27.

Tatoeba sentence data is made available under [CC BY 2.0 FR]. Audio is not
included. AudioRepeat stores only short text-and-translation pairs selected
for beginning practice; the original archive files are not committed.

[Tatoeba Project]: https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
[CC BY 2.0 FR]: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/deed.en

## Vocabulary provenance policy

Every newly generated vocabulary bank must have an identified source, license,
source URL, and attribution. The machine-readable registry lives at
`public/data/vocab-provenance/sources.json`; generated imports record their
exact source and selection rules alongside it. Publisher-owned coursebook and
word lists are not imported unless their redistribution terms are explicitly
cleared.
